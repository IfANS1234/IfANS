001 — WHO IS IfANS

IfANS is a British electronic musician & award-winning digital artist whose work has been shown at Raindance, Cannes, and beyond. He's supported pioneering acts such as Faithless and Leftfield. His debut album The Falling Wave is described by BBC 6 Music's Chris Hawkins as "Emotive Electronic Brilliance." IfANS is signed to Irvine Welsh's Out Yer Box.
